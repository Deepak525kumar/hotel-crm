## Purpose and Scope

**Outcome:** Establish Documents as the single, implementation-independent capability owning document lifecycle, document metadata, uploads, storage abstraction, version history, file status, document categories, document permissions, document access, document retrieval, document archival, and document relationships for every worker-facing and manager-facing document in the platform (uploaded onboarding documents, non-EU work-permit documents, and the scanned hand-signed employment contract as a **mechanism-class consumer**, per `RULE-HR-11`) — so that Onboarding, `backend-hr` (Contracts), Employee Management, and (forward-looking) Payslips/Compliance can reference one owner instead of each independently defining upload/storage behaviour. S3 (EU) object storage is implementation detail this capability owns operationally, not a capability it is defined by (CRR §4 line 53; CRR §7 lines 94–99; PDD §5.2 line 164, §5.7 line 195, §9.3 line 389).

**In scope:**

- **Current:** Nothing is implemented as a Documents capability. `backend/src/modules/documents` does not exist (directory `docs/03-modules/documents/` held only `.gitkeep` prior to this document). The single document-adjacent surface in the codebase — `POST /workers/:worker_id/documents` inside `backend/src/modules/hr/` — is recorded here **only as current-state evidence of the missing capability and of architectural debt** (see Evidence and Traceability, Migration Gaps `MIG-GAP-DOC-001`); it is not, and does not become, part of this module's owned surface, because it physically lives inside, and is scoped by, `backend-hr` (`SPEC-HR-001`), which explicitly disclaims owning the general capability (`docs/03-modules/hr/MODULE_SPEC.md:43,141` — "Documents (conceptual) owns general capability").
- **Target:** Document upload with expiry tracking at account creation (CRR §4 line 53). Worker upload of all required documents in the app during onboarding (CRR §7 line 96). Work-permit/residence document requirement restricted to non-EU/EEA/Swiss workers, with EU/EEA/Swiss citizens exempt (CRR §7 lines 97–98). Storage of all documents in S3, EU region, with no data leaving the EU/EEA (PDD §5.2 line 164; §5.7 line 195; §14 line 489). The `WorkerDocument` data model: "Uploaded docs + expiry; work-permit (non-EU only)" (PDD §9.3 line 389) — the sole target-state entity name CRR/PDD assign to this capability; there is no separate "Document" model in either authority.
- **Target (mechanism-class consumer relationship, not owned execution):** Serving as the storage/retrieval mechanism the contract-scan upload (`backend-hr`, `RULE-HR-11`) and the onboarding document-collection chatbot (Onboarding, CRR §8) both ride on, without Documents owning contract lifecycle (ADR-012) or onboarding orchestration (Onboarding's own workflow behaviour).

**Out of scope:** (owned elsewhere and referenced, never redefined — Constitution §6)

- **Contracts** — the entire contract-lifecycle/template/generation/metadata/version/amendment/signature/manager-confirmation/renewal/expiry/archival surface is owned by `backend-hr`/`SPEC-HR-001` per `ADR-012` (Accepted, 2026-07-12; `docs/09-decisions/architecture-decisions/ADR-012-contracts-ownership-hr-bounded-context.md`). Documents is named there explicitly as a **consumer**, not an owner, of Contracts (ADR-012 Decision item 3: "Onboarding, Employee Management, Documents, Payslips, Compliance... consume this functionality through `backend-hr`'s interfaces; they do not own it"). The scanned signed contract is *mechanically* uploaded like any other document (CRR §9 line 132) — that mechanism-sharing does not transfer contract ownership to Documents.
- **Payslips** — the payslip-request-to-manager-email flow (CRR §23) is owned by `backend-hr`/`SPEC-HR-001` (`docs/03-modules/hr/MODULE_SPEC.md:32`); `docs/03-modules/payslips/` holds only `.gitkeep` (no standalone module exists or is proposed here).
- **HR / employee records** — employee identity, core employment fields, lifecycle status, skill tags, and the profile-and-history view are owned by Employee Management (`docs/03-modules/employee-management/MODULE_SPEC.md`, `SPEC-EMP-001`), which explicitly lists "Document storage, expiry tracking, and the non-EU work-permit requirement" as owned by "the Documents module" and out of its own scope (`SPEC-EMP-001:39`).
- **Compliance** — automatic tiered-retention deletion execution, subject-rights access/export fulfilment, and special-category field governance policy are conceptually assigned to a Compliance capability (CRR §25, §26, §27); `docs/03-modules/compliance/` holds only `.gitkeep`. Documents classifies which retention tier its own records fall under (where determinable) and exposes retrieval for export; it does not execute deletion or fulfil subject-rights requests itself.
- **Consent** — the daily GDPR consent gate (CRR §24) is unrelated to document lifecycle; `docs/03-modules/consent/` holds only `.gitkeep`.
- **Chatbot** — the AI onboarding/subject-rights conversational agent (CRR §8, §26) is a distinct capability (`backend-chatbot`, currently a placeholder per PDD §2.2 line 44); Documents is the storage/retrieval target the chatbot's document-collection flow writes to and reads from, not the chatbot itself.
- **Geo** — geofencing/location capture (CRR/PDD §4.7, `backend-geo` placeholder) is unrelated.
- **CRM** — the Hotel/Hotel-Group reference record (`backend-crm`/`SPEC-CRM-001`, `ADR-011`) is unrelated; Documents does not scope by hotel beyond whatever hotel association a `WorkerDocument` inherits from its owning worker/employment record (undetermined — `OD-DOC-006`).
- **Onboarding orchestration** — the Personalfragebogen self-service form, the AI document-collection chatbot conversation flow, application→familiarization sequencing, and the pool/claim hire-approval mechanism are owned by Onboarding (`docs/03-modules/onboarding/MODULE_SPEC.md`), which itself explicitly disclaims owning document *validation*/*storage*: "Document validation itself is owned by the Documents module" (`onboarding/MODULE_SPEC.md:44`) and "Documents are stored by the Documents module; Onboarding maintains only references to them" (`onboarding/MODULE_SPEC.md:359`). Onboarding's own first-person claim to "document collection orchestration" (`onboarding/MODULE_SPEC.md:44,85`) is its own workflow-orchestration responsibility (deciding what to ask for and when, driving the chatbot conversation) — a consumer/dependency relationship with Documents, not a competing ownership claim over storage/lifecycle, and is recorded as such per the Lead Architect's G1.5 evidence.

**Non-goals:** (confirmed out of the platform entirely, or explicitly excluded by CRR/PDD)

- Any marketplace-era document/attachment concept — none existed in the marketplace-era data model (PDD §2.3 lines 45–56 list no document-bearing model) and none is reintroduced here.
- Certifications (formal, expiring qualifications) and certification-expiry reminders — explicitly excluded by CRR §4 (lines 54–55: "❌ No certifications... ❌ No certification expiry reminders"), a distinct concept from the confirmed general "document upload + expiry tracking" (CRR §4 line 53).
- Signature capture, signature authenticity verification, or automated detection that a document is signed — explicitly excluded for the contract-scan use case (CRR §9 line 139) and not requested for any other document type.
- Qualified Electronic Signature (QES) / e-signature provider integration of any kind (CRR §9 lines 118–119; PDD §11, Appendix).
- Payroll computation of any kind (CRR §23 lines 320–321) — Documents may store payroll-adjacent artifacts by reference (e.g., wage records classified Tier 3) but performs no computation.

## State and Lifecycle

**Current:** No state exists. No `WorkerDocument`/`Document` model, no lifecycle, no states, no transitions are implemented anywhere in the repository.

**Target lifecycle (reasoned from the confirmed capability boundary and CRR/PDD; states beyond "uploaded" and "expired" are not explicitly enumerated by either authority — labelled open, not assumed):**

- **Entry:** a `WorkerDocument` is created when a worker (or, for the contract-scan mechanism-class case, a manager) uploads a file (CRR §4 line 53, §7 line 96, §9 line 132) and it is persisted to S3 (EU) (PDD §5.2, §5.7, §8.1 line 321).
- **Expiry:** the record carries an expiry date (CRR §4 line 53: "Document upload + expiry tracking"); **the exact expiry-approach-on-crossing (warning schedule, blocking consequence, re-upload requirement) is unspecified by CRR/PDD** — contrast the fully specified contract expiry-reminder schedule (1-year/2-year, `hr/MODULE_SPEC.md` `REQ-HR-007`) which is `backend-hr`'s, not this module's (`OD-DOC-003`).
- **Categories:** CRR/PDD name only one confirmed document sub-type distinction — general document vs. work-permit document (non-EU only) (CRR §7 lines 97–98; PDD §9.3 line 389). No broader category taxonomy (e.g., ID document, proof-of-address, health document) is specified — `OD-DOC-005`.
- **Version history, permissions/access granularity, archival mechanics, and document relationships** — all four are named in this module's canonical boundary (given, not this document's to invent) but **CRR and PDD are silent on every one of them**. None is asserted as a requirement or acceptance criterion here (Constitution §6); each is recorded as an open decision/migration gap below, matching the pattern already established for other modules' silent-authority gaps (e.g., `SPEC-CRM-001` OD-CRM-01 Hotel Group data-model home, `SPEC-QUAL-001` OQ-08 tier/weighting thresholds).

**Concurrency:** not addressed anywhere in CRR/PDD for this capability — labelled unknown (`OD-DOC-011`), consistent with the same gap already recorded for `backend-hr` (`OD-HR-08`).

**Retention:** documents fall under one of CRR §25's three automatic-deletion tiers, but CRR/PDD do not explicitly state which. Reasoning (not a requirement): general uploaded onboarding documents most plausibly map to Tier 2 (5 years, "general personal/profile data," CRR §25 line 334) absent a more specific statement; work-permit documents and the contract-scan artifact could plausibly be argued Tier 3-adjacent (payroll/tax/legal-employment relevance, CRR §25 line 335) given their role in the legally mandatory employment-authorization record — **both readings are defensible from the text and neither is confirmed; this is `OD-DOC-001`, explicitly not resolved here** (mirrors `backend-hr`'s own identical unresolved tier question for the contract document itself, `OD-HR-11`).

## Failure, Security, Privacy, and Performance

**Failure modes/recovery:** Not evaluable for current state (nothing is implemented; the one stub route unconditionally 501s). Target: consistent with every other module's shared `AuditLog` pattern (`base-service.ts`) and with CRR §27's mandated per-view/edit audit-log entries for any special-category-classified field/document, though the latter's applicability to document types remains open (`OD-DOC-002`).

**Performance budgets/workload:** No explicit SLO is defined in CRR/PDD for document upload/retrieval latency or storage volume. Design intent: document volume scales with employee headcount and required-document count per worker (bounded, not request-rate-driven), similar in shape to `backend-hr`'s low-frequency, per-employee-per-year contract/payslip volume (`hr/MODULE_SPEC.md`'s own performance note).
