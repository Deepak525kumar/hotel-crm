## Purpose and Scope

**Outcome:** Establish Compliance as the single, standalone bounded context owning platform-wide **compliance governance, verification, reporting, policy evaluation, evidence generation, and regulatory workflows** — the orchestration and reporting layer that consumes state already owned by other modules (audit-log entries from `backend-auth`; consent records from `backend-consent`; deletion-audit entries from `backend-retention`; document retrievability from `backend-documents`) to produce governance artifacts, regulatory evidence, and subject-rights fulfilment, **without owning or writing any of the underlying source-of-truth state itself** (CRR §26, §27, §29, §30; `ADR-016`).

**In scope:**

- **Compliance governance:** platform-wide oversight of GDPR-adjacent policy application — coordinating (not itself performing) special-category data-handling policy (CRR §27), retention-tier policy oversight (the tier *definitions* and sweep *mechanism* remain `backend-retention`'s, per `SPEC-RETENTION-001`; Compliance's role is governance/reporting over the deletion-audit output, not tier design or sweep execution), and the daily GDPR consent-gate policy (owned end-to-end by `backend-consent` per `ADR-015`; Compliance consumes consent state for governance/audit purposes only).
- **Verification:** confirming, for governance-reporting purposes, that other modules' compliance-adjacent mechanisms (retention sweep completion, consent-gate enforcement, special-category access restrictions, audit-log completeness) are operating as those modules' own specifications describe — a read-only oversight function, not an enforcement mechanism Compliance itself executes.
- **Reporting:** producing compliance/regulatory reports and dashboards from data read across `backend-auth` (`AuditLog`), `backend-consent`, `backend-retention`, and `backend-documents`, per the interfaces those modules expose.
- **Policy evaluation:** assessing whether a given field, record type, or workflow's stated legal basis and data-handling posture (CRR §26's "each field tied to a stated legal basis") is documented and consistent — a documentation/governance activity, not a data-model or enforcement mechanism of its own.
- **Evidence generation:** assembling the audit trail and supporting artifacts a regulator, DPO, or internal reviewer would need (CRR §29's DPO-threshold tracking is a client-side action item, not this module's own mechanism — see Non-goals), sourced from `AuditLog` (read-only, via `backend-auth`), Consent's audit history, and Retention's deletion-audit log.
- **Regulatory workflows:** the GDPR subject-rights access/export workflow (CRR §26 — "handled automatically via a button or the chatbot"), which Compliance **orchestrates** (assembling the full response) while each source module (`backend-documents`, `backend-consent`, and any other worker-data-holding module) supplies only its own worker-scoped records through its own interfaces, per `docs/03-modules/documents/MODULE_SPEC.md` `RULE-DOC-10`/`OD-DOC-012`'s already-disclosed framing.
- **`AuditLog` consumption:** read-only access to `AuditLog` (`backend/prisma/schema.prisma:507-533`) via an interface `backend-auth` exposes (see `IF-COMPLIANCE-GetAuditTrail`, Interfaces below) — **Compliance never writes to `AuditLog`, never becomes its authoritative writer, and never duplicates its rows into a Compliance-owned table** (`ADR-016`).

**Out of scope:** (owned elsewhere and referenced, never redefined — Constitution §6; `ADR-016`)

- **`AuditLog` ownership, schema, lifecycle, and write API.** Exclusively `backend-auth`'s, per `ADR-016`. Compliance holds no write path to `AuditLog` under any circumstance.
- **Consent lifecycle, records, versions, withdrawal, renewal, audit history, and validation.** Exclusively `backend-consent`'s, per `ADR-015`. Compliance consumes Consent's audit history for governance/reporting only.
- **The retention tier-classification model, `RetentionLog`, and the daily automatic-deletion sweep job (mechanism, schedule, execution).** Exclusively `backend-retention`'s, per `docs/03-modules/retention/MODULE_SPEC.md`'s own G1.5 PASS and Ownership and Boundaries section. Compliance reads Retention's deletion-audit log (`IF-RETENTION-GetDeletionAuditLog`) for governance/reporting purposes only; it does not decide tier assignment, does not run the sweep, and does not own `RetentionLog`.
- **Document lifecycle, metadata, uploads, storage, and retrieval.** Exclusively `backend-documents`'s (target, `docs/03-modules/documents/MODULE_SPEC.md`). Compliance's subject-rights orchestration reads Documents' worker-scoped retrievability surface (`RULE-DOC-10`) but does not own or redefine document state.
- **Contract lifecycle, templates, generation, signatures, and the payslip-request-to-fulfilment capability.** Exclusively `backend-hr`'s, per `ADR-012`/`ADR-014`. Not referenced by CRR/PDD as a Compliance responsibility, and no sibling specification claims Compliance owns it.
- **Chatbot/AI-execution capability.** Exclusively `backend-chatbot`'s, per `ADR-013`. Where the chatbot is the *delivery mechanism* for a subject-rights request (CRR §8, §26), Compliance consumes Chatbot's interfaces to initiate/receive that interaction; Compliance does not own conversation lifecycle, prompts, or AI orchestration.
- **The Art. 28 GDPR Data Processing Agreement (DPA) between the vendor (Zirove) and the client (FHM Hotelservice GmbH), and the mandatory Data Protection Officer (DPO) threshold tracking under §38 BDSG.** CRR §29 explicitly assigns both as client/business-side `[ACTION]` items, not application mechanisms — "the client must track and own this (owner: client side, not the app)." Compliance discloses these as governance context; it implements no DPA-tracking or DPO-threshold-monitoring feature, absent a future confirmed requirement.
- **Special-category *field-level* visibility restriction and per-view/edit audit-entry mechanics for any specific module's data** (Konfession, disability, health-data fields; CRR §27). The restricted-visibility permission tier and the per-access audit-log entry are each owning module's own mechanism to implement on its own fields (mirroring `docs/03-modules/documents/MODULE_SPEC.md` `RULE-DOC-07`'s identical framing for document *types*); Compliance's role is policy governance/oversight that such handling exists and is applied consistently, not implementing the field-level mechanism itself.
- **Payroll/salary calculation, overtime, bonuses, deductions.** CRR §23 confirms these are explicitly not handled by the system at all (manual, manager-side); not a Compliance concern.

**Non-goals:** (confirmed out of scope entirely, or explicitly excluded by CRR/PDD)

- An admin-facing audit-log viewer/search screen — CRR §30 confirms "No admin-facing log viewer/search screen (admin does not want a log viewer)"; Compliance's reporting surface is not a general-purpose log browser.
- Data minimization/active pruning of collected fields — CRR §26 confirms "we don't need minimization," though each field must remain tied to a stated legal basis (a policy-evaluation activity this module does perform, distinct from minimization).
- Any second, competing audit-log writer, consent-lifecycle owner, retention sweep, or document store inside this module — explicitly and permanently excluded by the resolved ownership boundaries above (`ADR-012`, `ADR-013`, `ADR-014`, `ADR-015`, `ADR-016`, and `SPEC-RETENTION-001`'s own G1.5 PASS).
- Implementing the DPA/DPO client-side action items as application features, absent a future confirmed requirement (see Out of scope).

## Ownership and Boundaries

**Module owner:** `unassigned` — accountable owner assignment is reserved human authority (`SYNC-001`; no `CODEOWNERS`, empty `backend/package.json` author, consistent with every other module in this repository). Code-level home is the target `backend-compliance` module (`backend/src/modules/compliance`), which does not exist today in any form — not a stub, not a `.placeholder` file. This specification is the proposed docs↔code mapping for `backend-compliance`; it is not asserted as final until G2 freeze.

**Owned state:**

- None confirmed today beyond whatever governance-artifact/report state a future revision may define (e.g., a generated-report or evidence-package record, if CRR/PDD or a future confirmed requirement names one) — `[OPEN]`, `OD-COMPLIANCE-002`. Compliance's authoring evidence (CRR §26, §27, §29, §30) describes orchestration and reporting responsibilities, not a confirmed persisted entity of its own.

**Explicitly NOT owned (per `ADR-016` and the sibling ADRs cited throughout this document):**

- `AuditLog` (`state-audit-log`) — owned by `backend-auth`. Compliance does **not** own `AuditLog` state, lifecycle, or write APIs (`ADR-016`, confirming the task-supplied instruction verbatim).
- Consent lifecycle/records/versions/withdrawal/renewal/audit history/validation — owned by `backend-consent` (`ADR-015`).
- Retention tier definitions, `RetentionLog`, and the sweep mechanism — owned by `backend-retention` (`docs/03-modules/retention/MODULE_SPEC.md`).
- Document lifecycle/metadata/storage/retrieval — owned by `backend-documents` (target).
- Contract lifecycle and the payslip-request-to-fulfilment capability — owned by `backend-hr` (`ADR-012`, `ADR-014`).
- Chatbot/AI-execution capability — owned by `backend-chatbot` (`ADR-013`).

**Consumed state (owned elsewhere, referenced, never redefined):**

- `AuditLog` — read-only, via `backend-auth`'s interface (`IF-COMPLIANCE-GetAuditTrail`, target-state).
- Consent records and audit history — read-only, via `backend-consent`'s interfaces (`IF-CONSENT-GetAuditHistory`, per `docs/03-modules/consent/MODULE_SPEC.md`).

## Failure, Security, Privacy, and Performance

**Failure modes/recovery:**

- A source module (`backend-auth`, `backend-consent`, `backend-retention`, `backend-documents`) is unavailable when a subject-rights bundle is assembled → no confirmed partial-failure/retry/idempotency behavior exists in CRR/PDD; labelled unknown — `[OPEN]`, `OD-COMPLIANCE-005`.
- `backend-auth`'s `AuditLog`-read interface does not yet exist → this module's reporting requirements remain target-state only until that dependency is implemented (see Dependencies).
- Current-state: this module does not exist in code; every interface listed above is target-state only.

**Trust boundaries/authorization:** every `IF-COMPLIANCE-*` interface's calling-actor-identity parameter MUST be derived from the calling actor's own already-authenticated session context — never accepted as an unverified client-supplied field — mirroring the precedent set by `docs/03-modules/hr/MODULE_SPEC.md`'s `RULE-HR-14`, `docs/03-modules/consent/MODULE_SPEC.md`'s and `docs/03-modules/retention/MODULE_SPEC.md`'s identical treatment of their own respective interface families. **Security gap, disclosed not silently accepted:** the "Admin/DPO-equivalent" caller class named for `IF-COMPLIANCE-GetGovernanceReport` has no defined RBAC scope, role model, or authorization boundary anywhere in this specification — mirroring `docs/03-modules/retention/MODULE_SPEC.md`'s `OD-RETENTION-05` finding for its own equivalent interface. Until `OD-COMPLIANCE-006` is resolved with an explicit permission/scope definition, **no implementation may grant "Admin/DPO-equivalent" access to `IF-COMPLIANCE-GetGovernanceReport`**. Separately, Compliance's own actions (subject-rights fulfilment, report generation) are not yet confirmed to themselves produce `AuditLog` entries — a self-referential gap (would Compliance's own governance activity be audited by the very `AuditLog` it reads?) tracked as `OD-COMPLIANCE-008`, unresolved by CRR/PDD.

**Data classification/retention:** Germany-only, consistent with every other module in this platform; no data leaves the EU/EEA. This module reads, but never independently persists a durable copy of, `AuditLog`/Consent/Retention/Documents data — every subject-rights bundle it assembles is a point-in-time composition, not a Compliance-owned duplicate store, avoiding the same "un-retained copy of the very data it protects" risk `docs/03-modules/retention/MODULE_SPEC.md` explicitly designed around for its own deletion-audit log. Special-category field handling (CRR §27) remains each owning module's own mechanism; this module's oversight role does not itself store special-category data.
