## New defects found

1. **`PUT /calendar/hotels/:hotel_id/shift-summaries/:date` returns a raw `500` instead of
   `422` on a malformed body** — `backend/src/modules/calendar/shift-summary/routes.ts` — LOW
   severity (no data leak — the 500 body was already generic — but a real correctness/UX bug: a
   manager who mistypes or omits a field on this form gets `"An unexpected error occurred"`
   with no indication of what was wrong). Root cause: the route called
   `dailyShiftSummarySchema.parse(req.body)` inline instead of this codebase's established
   `validateBody()` middleware, which is the only thing that converts a `ZodError` into a
   proper `ValidationError` — the global error handler has no `ZodError` branch of its own.
   **Fixed in this pass**, in `fix/manager-queue-guard-and-reactivate-scope` (PR #622):
   `validateBody(dailyShiftSummarySchema)` added as route middleware, matching every sibling
   endpoint. Verified live (malformed body → proper `422` with field detail; happy path
   unchanged) and against the full serial suite (148 suites, 3571 tests, all passing).

No other new defects found this pass — everything else exercised (scenarios 04, 11, and the
bulk of 12) matched documented/expected behavior exactly, including several assertions that
exist specifically to catch IDOR-class bugs (12 Step 3) or total-lockout-class bugs (11 Step
1), which is the highest-value thing this kind of pass can confirm.
